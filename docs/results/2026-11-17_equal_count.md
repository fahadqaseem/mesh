# Equal-count comparison: TSDF vs TSDF + gap fill at exactly 1,000,000 points

Fahad Qaseem Khawar, 17 Nov 2026. Generated by `scripts/equal_count.py`. Set A = 7 original shapes, set B = 12 new shapes (clean test set); pencilpot is the dev shape and is in no mean. Mean ± std over shapes.

**Method.** Both clouds are subsampled to exactly 1,000,000 points, uniformly at random without replacement (`numpy.random.default_rng(0)`). The TSDF cloud already has exactly 1M points, so for it the draw only reorders them; the gap-filled cloud (1.01M to 1.33M points) loses its excess at random, so the share of kernel points stays as in the full cloud. Then protocol v1 (`docs/methods.md` section 9): Chamfer / Hausdorff x100 on 5,000 points, F1 / NC on 100,000, recall with all points. **Outliers** = share of points whose exact distance to the GT surface (Open3D `RaycastingScene.compute_distance` on the GT triangles) exceeds 0.01 or 0.02 in the normalised frame (GT largest extent = 1, so 0.01 = 1% of the object's size). This is 100 minus precision with all points, measured against the exact surface instead of 100k surface samples.

## Findings

- **The gap fill's gains are not a density artefact.** At exactly 1M points each, the gap-filled cloud still has the
  better Chamfer (set B 5.33 vs 5.58, better on 9 of 12; set A 5.43 vs 5.57, 5 of 7), the better F1@0.02 (+0.023, 10 of 12)
  and far better recall with all points (@0.02 0.825 vs 0.682, 12 of 12). F1@0.01 and NC are unchanged within 0.005.
- **But it adds outliers on every shape.** Points more than 0.02 from the GT surface: set B 24.0% to 28.0% (+4.0 points,
  worse on 12 of 12), set A 20.4% to 23.8% (worse on 7 of 7); beyond 0.01: +2.8 points on set B. The added kernel points
  cover holes but many sit 1-3% off the surface. Task C (`docs/results/2026-11-18_outliers.md`) looks for filters.
- **The TSDF cloud itself is far from clean**: on set B, 50% of its points are more than 1% of the object's size from
  the GT and 24% more than 2% (set A 44% and 20%). These come from VGGT's depth errors and the camera-only alignment
  (no ICP), not from the gap fill, and they vary widely by shape (monsterring 1.2% beyond 0.01, thingi_103356 85%).
- The outlier metric uses the exact point-to-triangle distance; it agrees with 100 - precision with all points
  (protocol v1's P_all, against 100k surface samples) up to the sampling gap (wheel_76903: 22.2% vs 24.4%), and the
  exact distance was checked to be at most, and within 0.002 of, the distance to 2M surface samples.
