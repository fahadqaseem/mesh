## 2. GT-free joint fit of depth scale and focal

**Method** (`src/photo2mesh/points/depth_scale.py` `fit_joint`, `scripts/depth_scale.py --joint`). Same multi-view agreement cost as the 18 Nov depth scale (truncated mean relative depth residual between all ordered view pairs, bilinear lookup, eps 0.005), now with two parameters for all views: depth scale k and focal factor phi (focal = phi x the known render focal of 711.6 px; principal point fixed at the centre). The fused pixels are back-projected with phi f and projected into the other views with phi f; VGGT's camera poses stay fixed. Grid k 0.94-1.08 and phi 0.88-1.06 (step 0.01), then step 0.0025 around the best. Fused with the default TSDF using both (`tsdf_points.py --depth-scale --focal-scale`). No GT anywhere in the fit. Unit test: a synthetic sphere given a focal 1/0.95 too long and depth x 0.97 is recovered to 0.01 (`tests/test_depth_scale.py`). Reference without a fit: VGGT's own predicted focal with its depth (`intrinsics: predicted`), also GT-free.

**Result on pencilpot: the joint fit fails, and no eps rescues it.** The views agree best at phi = 0.925, which is
VGGT's own focal estimate (0.923 of the true one): VGGT's depth, poses and focal are consistent with each other, so the
agreement cost cannot tell its focal from the true one. With that focal the object is ~8% too wide and everything gets
much worse (Chamfer 6.19 to 8.19, outliers > 0.02 22% to 70%), almost as bad as simply using VGGT's focal. eps 0.005 is
kept for set B only for consistency with the 18 Nov fit.

**Interpretation.**
- **The joint fit is worse on every shape** (Chamfer 5.58 to 7.91, recall all @0.02 0.682 to 0.446, outliers > 0.02
  23.9% to 52.6%, 0 of 12 better). On every shape the fitted focal lands on VGGT's own estimate (mean 0.929 vs 0.923,
  correlation 0.89; thingi_84620 hit the grid edge at 0.88, its VGGT focal is 0.888), and the depth scale goes back to
  ~1.00. Using VGGT's own focal without a fit gives nearly the same (8.10).
- **What this says about the bias:** VGGT returns a self-consistent world (depth, poses and focal agree with each other)
  whose focal is 4-11% too short. Multi-view agreement can only find that world, not the true one, so a GT-free fit of
  the focal from VGGT's own outputs is not possible. The known focal is what makes our clouds metrically right; the
  remaining +0.0065 offset likely comes from forcing the true focal onto depth that was predicted for VGGT's focal:
  across set B, the shorter VGGT's focal, the larger the outward offset (correlation -0.46 between VGGT focal / known
  and the signed offset; moderate, 12 shapes, so supporting evidence rather than proof). The depth
  scale alone (18 Nov) partly compensates; correcting it properly needs information VGGT does not provide: a known
  focal given to VGGT itself (it has no input for it), or a different depth model that accepts intrinsics.
- **Decision:** keep the known focal and unscaled depth as the default; drop the GT-free focal fit.
