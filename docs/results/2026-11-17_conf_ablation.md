**Summary (for Friday)**
- **Alignment is not the problem; the depth is biased.** Camera centres fit the GT cameras to 0.7% of the orbit radius
  (RMS), so the camera scale is right to well under 1%. Rigid ICP (diagnostic, uses GT) removes only 6.7 points of the
  24% outliers > 0.02. ICP with a free scale removes 17 points (to 7%), but the scale it fits (0.970 ± 0.011, below 1 on
  12 of 12) is not a camera error: it absorbs an outward offset of the surface. VGGT's fused depth pixels already sit
  **outside** the GT surface (median +0.012 of the object size, 80% of pixels outside, i.e. the depth is ~1% too short),
  and TSDF fusion halves that (+0.0065). So the outliers are a VGGT depth bias, not alignment.
- **conf_percentile 30 and 10 change nothing on 11 of 12 shapes.** The percentile is taken over all pixels, and VGGT's
  foreground covers less than half the image, so the 50th percentile is already the background value 1.0; the binding
  filter is VGGT's own foreground (conf > 1.0). Only thingi_103356 (55% foreground) gains pixels (+5%), and 30 and 10 are
  identical there too. Set B means move by at most 0.01 Chamfer / 0.01 recall.
- **Extra depth pixels do not fill the gaps; Gaussians do (with outliers).** Even fusing every foreground pixel (conf 10
  and no 2 px erosion, +6% pixels, 74% of them > 0.02 off) fills 4% of the GT gap area and makes Chamfer worse
  (+0.20); gap fill fills 46% (Chamfer -0.25, recall all @0.02 +0.14 on 12 of 12) at +4 points of outliers. The gaps are
  surfaces no view sees (94% of gap points lie behind a fused surface or on background in every view), so no pixel
  filter can reach them.

**Interpretation.**
- Rigid ICP moves the cloud by 0.6 deg and 1.3% of the object size and lowers outliers > 0.02 from 23.9% to 17.3%: that
  much is pose error (VGGT's camera directions plus the Umeyama fit). It is real but not the main part.
- Scale is the main part, and it is not a camera-scale error: the camera centres agree with the GT rig to 0.7% RMS of
  the orbit radius (`align/sim3.json` camera_rms), far below the 3% ICP asks for. A cloud whose surface is pushed
  outward by d looks to ICP like a slightly larger cloud (about 1 + 2d for a convex object of extent 1, more where parts
  are thin or close to the centre). The signed offsets have the right sign on 12 of 12 shapes and the same order of
  size (+0.0065 on average, +0.014 and +0.020 on thingi_593381 and 103356, the shapes ICP-with-scale helps most); the
  match is qualitative, not an exact decomposition.
- Where the offset comes from: section 2 measures the fused pixels before the TSDF. They are already outside the
  surface (median +0.012, 80% outside on average). So VGGT's depth head places surfaces ~1% too close to the camera
  relative to its own camera poses; fusion averages views from opposite sides and halves the offset.
- Implication: protocol v1 numbers are not inflated by our alignment. A GT-free correction is possible in principle
  (rescale the depth maps by one factor that maximises agreement between views) and is the next thing to try.

**Interpretation: do the extra depth pixels fill the gaps better than Gaussians? No.**
- The conf 50 cloud misses 32% of the GT surface by more than 0.02. Lower confidence thresholds fill 2% of that gap on
  average (all from thingi_103356), every foreground pixel fills 4%; gap fill fills 46%.
- Why: the gaps are not where pixels were filtered out. Of the 18 Nov gap points that no view has depth for, 99.4% lie
  behind a fused surface in at least one view and 94% are behind fused depth or on background in every view that sees
  them; only 0.3% depend only on rim pixels (re-checked on the saved Task C depth maps). They are surfaces the 8 cameras
  never see (undersides, concavities). The Gaussians reach them because 3DGS fills unseen volume to explain the
  silhouettes, which is also why most of what they add is off the surface.
- So the outlier and gap problems have different causes: the outliers of the TSDF cloud come from VGGT's depth bias
  (section 1), the gaps from unobserved surface. The levers are a depth-scale correction for the first and more or
  better-placed views (16 views raised recall on 12 of 12) for the second, not the confidence threshold.
- Recommendation: leave conf_percentile at 50 (it is inert) and keep the 2 px erosion.
